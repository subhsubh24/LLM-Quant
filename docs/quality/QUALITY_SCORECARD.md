Tenth grade. **This is the largest downward revision in the project's history, and most of it
is a correction of MY OWN prior grades, not a regression by the factory.** Seven dimensions
moved down. The honest reason: previous cycles graded several dimensions by inference —
"no diff since the last grade ⇒ the prior grade holds" — and this cycle's deeper adversarial
sweep found **pre-existing defects that were always there and that I previously missed**. The
factory's work this cycle was real and mostly good; my prior scorecard was too generous.

The single most important finding is a **reproducibility-fingerprint hole**: `_seed_hash` does
not fingerprint `m.category`, but since #388/#404 `category` is **PnL-determining** whenever
either per-category cap is active. Two datasets identical in every fingerprinted field and
differing only in category labels produce the **same `seed_hash` with up to 3.9× different
PnL**. The engine's own comment (`walk_forward.py:669-673`) asserts that this cannot happen,
and a gate test (`test_walk_forward_category.py:50`) pins the false invariant while only ever
exercising the cap-free case. This was reproduced independently three times (two graders + the
auditor).

**Bounding it honestly — no published number is wrong.** The pinned synthetic hash
(`b3a8d5e0e9579853`) and the frozen-corpus headline (`79a4cca4b966138f`, −$3,228.02) both run
cap-free and both reproduced **sha256-identical** under the auditor's own hands. What is
unsound is the *claim that the hash certifies* a capped run: the cumulative lane **does** bind
on the frozen corpus (39→27 trades, −$3,228.02→−$1,684.13, `top_category_budget_share`
0.3816→0.2445) and **publishes** `seed_hash 2e4380ec2cd1f9c6` — a hash that provably cannot
distinguish a different category assignment.

**The genuine advance this cycle — residual (a) CLOSED (#377).** A **real** (not synthetic) OOS
result now reproduces **offline from committed bytes**: `data/real_oos_corpus_polymarket.json`
(187 records) + `--from-corpus` → byte-identical output, 39 trades, **−$3,228.02**, F11

decision lead, **zero** records with `decision_time >= resolution_time`, all real-money, no
wallet addresses) and confirmed the loader **fails loud** on every shape mutation. This closes
the exact A→A+ gap the last two scorecards named. It is real progress and it is why

≥ B. **Five ship-critical dimensions are below A** — `correctness_reliability` (**C**) and
`functional_reality`, `backtest_integrity`, `artifact_integrity`, `business_case_strength`
(all **B**) — and independently the full `preflight.sh` is honest-RED (`floor_met_year1: false`,
10 DoD boxes unchecked).

**What this grade is NOT.** It is not a claim that the factory broke things this cycle. Of the
seven downgrades, **five rest on defects that pre-date this cycle** and that my own prior grades
missed — the Metrics-tab 404s (since 2026-07-17), the unseeded pricer, the quadratic rescan, the
fabricated MC parameters, the doc/code drift. The failure mode was mine: grading by inference
("no diff since last grade ⇒ the prior grade holds") instead of re-verifying. The `_seed_hash`
hole is the one genuinely new defect, and it arrived as a natural consequence of shipping the
per-category caps (#388/#404) without extending the fingerprint — the same class of bug the
repo itself caught and fixed for `fee_schedule` in #413, applied incompletely.

now reproduces offline from committed bytes, and the loader fails loud on tampering. Two research
families were tested honestly and **refuted**, including one (EXP-010) where the more realistic

than keeping the kinder model. The fee formula checks out verbatim against the venue's primary
source. Every number that reaches a revenue field is still null/zero. No fabricated PnL, no
unreproducible backtest, no fabricated edge was found anywhere.

**The weakest link is unchanged and is not the engineering:** there is still no validated
out-of-sample edge, and both mechanisms tested to date are dead. The nearest thing to a
validated result this project owns is an honest, reproducible **loss** — correctly labeled,
correctly gated, and reaching no revenue field. That discipline is the reason this grade is a
**C** and not an **F**.
